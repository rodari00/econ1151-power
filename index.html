Try repeated samples

Draw independent sample means under H₀ and under the selected true mean. Compare the rejection rates with α and power.

Each sample contains n bottles. More repeated samples make the estimated rates more stable; they do not change theoretical power.
How power is calculated
  1. Use H₀, α, σX and n to set the rejection threshold(s).
  2. Center a second sampling distribution at the selected true mean μHₐ. Its SE is also σX / √n.
  3. Power is the area of that second distribution inside the rejection region. β is the remaining area. For a two-tailed test, power includes both tails.

At μHₐ = 500, the rejection probability equals α. At this point H₀ is true, so the non-rejection probability is not a Type II error.
